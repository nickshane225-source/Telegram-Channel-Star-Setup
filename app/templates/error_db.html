MongoDB Atlas Unreachable

StarPost Manager could not establish a connection to your MongoDB instance. If you are using MongoDB Atlas, the most common reason is that your current public IP address is not added to your Atlas IP Access List.

How to fix this in MongoDB Atlas:

  1. Log in to your MongoDB Atlas Console.
  2. In the left sidebar under Security, click Network Access.
  3. Click the green "Add IP Address" button.
  4. Choose "Add Current IP Address" (or select "Allow Access From Anywhere (0.0.0.0/0)" for testing).
  5. Click Confirm and wait ~1 minute for Atlas to update your firewall rule.
  6. Verify your MONGODB_URI in .env is formatted correctly.
🔄 Retry Connection Make sure your PC has an active internet connection.
