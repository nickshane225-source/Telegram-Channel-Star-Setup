Mode A vs Mode B Quick Guide
🖼️ Mode A: Locked Text Card
  • How buyers see it: Text is auto-rendered on an image card appended to the channel album. Unlocks directly in Telegram.

  • How buyers see it: Channel sees blurred teaser with 'Unlock' link. Full media and text are delivered in bot DM.
  • Where Stars go: Credited directly to the Bot's balance.
  • Requires: StarPost Manager running on PC/server with Bot Listener enabled.
  • Editing: Price and hidden text can be edited anytime without reposting!
